Our Commitment to Transparency

Vegas ALL believes that transparency builds trust. We make important organizational, tax exempt, and financial information available to help our donors, sponsors, partners, volunteers, and community understand our organization and its work.

Our Mission

Vegas ALL Non-Profit Corporation serves the local Las Vegas community and, as resources permit, supports carefully supervised charitable activities internationally — uniting people through sports, outreach, and nourishment.

Community Impact

Our four pillars of impact are Feeding, Fundraising, Wellness & Recreation, and Community Outreach. Through accessible community wellness programs, charitable assistance, hunger-relief initiatives, and community-driven events, Vegas ALL uses pickleball and sport to build connection and turn play into purpose.
